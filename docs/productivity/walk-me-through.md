## What it does

`walk-me-through` tutors one list, one item per turn, in plain words, and checks every claim against the source. The session is read-only until the person ends it and asks for a change.

## When to reach for it

Type `/walk-me-through` with an issue, a pull request, a spec, a doc, or a topic that has parts. If the topic has no list yet, the skill proposes 4 to 10 items and waits before teaching the first one. Only you invoke it.
