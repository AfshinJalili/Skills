## What it does

`whiteboard-defense` applies Mitchell Hashimoto's whiteboard defense before customer-facing work counts as shipped. The person who ships it has to be able to explain how the system works and defend the decisions, without remembering function names.

The defense is one board: the path, the alternative that lost, the hostile path, what is stored, and where it breaks. A caption says why the alternative lost and names any gap. A paragraph that describes a drawing is not a drawing.

## When to reach for it

The agent reaches for it when work is about to ship, merge, or release, and when you ask why this instead of that, what a malicious actor can do, which representation and why, where it fails, or for a whiteboard of a feature.

A PoC, demo, spike, or experiment is exempt. The skill says so in one line and gets out of the way. Speed wins there.
